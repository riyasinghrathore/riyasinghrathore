Most interesting things happen at the edges.

In ecology, this is known as the **[edge effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_effect)**: where two ecosystems meet, like a forest and a meadow, the boundary often supports a greater diversity of life than either ecosystem alone.

I tend to find myself there too. My formal training is in **Applied Geophysics at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad**, with a minor in **Mathematics and Statistics**. My skills are a little meadow of mathematics and computer science that I've Frankensteined together with the things I've learned from my forest.

Today, I'm a **Software Engineer at Salesforce**, working on **[Lightning Out 2.0](https://salesforce-specialist.medium.com/lightning-out-2-0-salesforce-just-rewrote-the-rules-for-embedding-components-d24da4540661)** (a platform for embedding live, interactive components into external web pages). It's a deceptively deep problem, and building it has been some of the best applied education in web security I could have asked for. I'm hoping to write up its architecture, once I find the time.

In another universe, I'd be a **professional skier**.

In this one, I don't actually know how to ski.
